# Feature evidence and reported monetary detail — B65, 2026-11-03

Platform capability declarations describe implemented mappings, not installation or checkout acceptance. Every feature now includes `evidence_id`, `source_url`, `error_code`, `last_verified_at` and `evidence_scope`. A declaration has no observation ID/date; its HTTPS source is reference documentation. Unsupported features retain their explicit error code. A global live-verification date cannot prove every feature.

Connection reports use current credential-generation observations. Catalog features link the latest corresponding sync; partial/failed passes retain their evidence ID and error and supersede older successes. Category evidence covers categories assigned to synced products only. Native orders, payments and cash refunds require their own result flags and a successful recovery bound to workspace, current credentials, domain and provider store. Order completeness alone cannot establish payment/refund support. Refund status transitions without a positive reported refund and refund timestamp cannot establish cash-refund evidence. Old records without the additional flag remain unverified until a fresh observation. Authenticated webhook receipt is scoped as receipt evidence, never full checkout acceptance. Paused/disconnected/unverified-ownership connections cannot advertise current availability. Features without matching evidence remain LIMITED, even if the connection label is Connected.

Evidence source URLs resolve authenticated existing event/sync list endpoints with an exact optional `evidence_id` ULID. They contain no token, payload or provider customer data. Scope authorization still applies: foreign workspace404, malformed filter422, valid unrelated evidence ID200/empty list. These links retrieve sanitized records, not native provider archives. `error_code=null` means that observation succeeded; it does not assert installation, storefront, uninstall or overall release acceptance.

Money remains integer minor units with explicit currency and unknown parts null:

- Shopware and Magento already retain strict order-bound flat product lines and reported monetary detail. Existing mappings are preserved; composite/unbound/ambiguous lines are refused.
- Ecwid reports current `total`, `tax`, `shippingOption.shippingRate`, `discount` and `pricesIncludeTax`. Do not sum `couponDiscount` into `discount`. Reported `refundedAmount` remains separate; no subtraction is repeated. Original immutable total, settlement time and cash-refund time remain unknown. Currency comes from authenticated shop settings at read time, not an immutable per-order currency claim.
- Lightspeed C reports documented `priceIncl` as gross total using authenticated shop currency. Tax/shipping/discount/refund cash details and settlement time remain null; returns/credit invoices are not cash reimbursement proof.
- Shopify reads `currentShippingPriceSet` into the distinct `current_shipping_minor`, after refunds and discounts. Original `shipping_minor` remains null. Original/current totals and reported refund amounts stay separate; current shipping is never added to or deducted from the canonical gross total. A different currency, excessive precision, negative or missing part remains null.

Primary field references reviewed: [Ecwid order](https://docs.ecwid.com/api-reference/rest-api/orders/get-order), [Shopify Order](https://shopify.dev/docs/api/admin-graphql/2026-10/objects/Order). Shopify includes tax in current shipping only when `taxesIncluded` is true. Original tax and current shipping therefore represent explicitly different provider snapshots; the breakdown is not an additive invoice reconstruction.
