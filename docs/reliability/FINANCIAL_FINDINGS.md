## B22-F005 — zero commission left a false pending state (medium, fixed 2026-10-28)

Reproduced with a positive pending reward followed by network approval at zero commission: pending wallet value was cancelled, but affiliate reward_status stayed pending. Regression failed expected cancelled/actual pending. Approval now atomically sets reward_amount0/reward_status cancelled without a ledger credit; pending source integrity is also checked by reconciliation. Historical credits and owner-approved spendability are unchanged. Canonical regression subsequently passed in the focused suite; full-suite/CI evidence is recorded in PROGRESS.
